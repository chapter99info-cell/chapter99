Our Story

"Every dish is crafted with authentic Thai flavour and 100% halal-certified ingredients — a taste of home for the whole neighbourhood."

10+Years in Bondi
4.8Uber Eats rating
78Dishes on the menu

Mama Made Roti started as a family kitchen with one belief: good Thai food should feel like home. Every curry is simmered slow, every roti hand-folded to order, and every plate certified 100% halal — from the saffron biryani to the tamarind king prawn.

Gluten-free and vegan options run through the entire menu, clearly marked so nobody has to guess — dine in, take away, or have it delivered straight to your door.
